My Favorite City

My favorite city is Logan, Utah, USA. Logan is my favorite city because it has a small-town feel, but it also offers many of the services and options for food and entertainment you usually find in larger cities. I also love the beautiful mountains that surround the valley.

I also love Logan because it's where I met my husband and where we spent most of our time together while we courted. We got engaged in Logan, and we were sealed together in the Logan temple. I hope we can live in the Logan area at least until our youngest graduates high school, so for at least 15 more years.
