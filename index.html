Utah

Utah is the 45th state admitted to the United States on January 4th, 1896. It is the 13th-largest state. Its capital and largest city is Salt Lake City. The name "Utah" comes from the Ute Native American tribe and means "people of the mountains." Utah has a variety of biomes, most commonly low-elevation deserts, semiarid landscapes, wetlands, sagebrush steppes, forests, and alpine tundras. Utah's elevation ranges from 2,000 ft (610 m) to 13,534 ft (4,120 m).
